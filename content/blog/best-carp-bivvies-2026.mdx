## What to Look for in a Carp Bivvy

Before spending money, consider these factors:

- **Session length:** Day sessions need portability; overnighters need space and insulation
- **Size:** Can you sit upright? Is there room for your bed, kit bag, and a brew?
- **Waterproofing (HH rating):** 5,000mm minimum for UK rain; 10,000mm+ for serious weather
- **Ventilation:** Condensation build-up ruins sessions — mesh inner panels help
- **Setup time:** Some bivvies take 15 minutes to erect; others 2
- **Weight and pack size:** If you walk long distances to swims, this matters a lot

## Bivvy Setup Tips

1. **Always peg out in windy conditions** — even a well-made bivvy will collapse in strong wind if not pegged properly
2. **Face away from the prevailing wind** — keeps rain off the door opening
3. **Leave ventilation slightly open overnight** — reduces condensation dramatically
4. **Use a groundsheet inside as well** — even bivvies with built-in groundsheets benefit from an inner layer for insulation
5. **Check your poles before every session** — cracked or bent poles make setup unreliable
